Aviator on Yaarwin: Crash Game Guide for Beginners

Aviator is a multiplier “crash” game: a plane climbs and the multiplier rises until it flies away. Cash out before the crash to lock profit. It is the second most searched game on Yaarwin after Wingo and appears on most colour-prediction apps in India (Spribe-style mechanics).

How Aviator Works

  1. Place one or two bets before the round starts.
  2. Watch the multiplier (1.00x upward).
  3. Tap cash out at your target (e.g. 1.50x, 2.00x).
  4. If the plane crashes before you cash out, that bet is lost.

Beginner Strategy (Risk Control)

  • Target low multipliers (1.2x–1.8x) for higher hit rate, smaller wins.
  • Use auto cash-out to remove emotion in fast rounds.
  • Split bet: one conservative cash-out, one higher risk — optional.
  • Never chase losses; Aviator variance is sharper than colour bets.
